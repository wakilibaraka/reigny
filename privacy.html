Privacy Policy & Client Terms

Last updated: October 2026 — Nairobi, Kenya

1. Information Governance

Reigny Creative Agency collects only essential data necessary to deliver bespoke creative services, respond to project inquiries, and analyze aggregate digital performance. We do not sell, rent, or lease client data to third parties under any circumstances.

2. Intellectual Property Rights

All bespoke brand identities, custom codebases, film assets, and art direction materials created for contracted clients transfer in full ownership upon complete settlement of invoices, subject to mutual agreement granting Reigny showcase and archival rights.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We frequently execute preliminary NDAs prior to accessing unreleased product details, business metrics, or strategic roadmaps. Strict digital partitioning safeguards client collateral throughout every production lifecycle.

4. Governing Jurisdiction

Unless explicitly specified within a bespoke master services agreement (MSA), contractual terms are interpreted in accordance with the laws of Kenya, alongside international commercial dispute arbitration standards.
