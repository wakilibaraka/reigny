Neon Noir
Darkness engineered with light.

Client Noir Spirits Luxury Group
Timeline 8-Week Sprint
Capabilities Direction, 4K Cinema, Web
Headline Metric +240% Engagement Surge
[The Dilemma]

Luxury spirits brands had become visually sterile.

Noir Spirits approached Reigny to launch their limited reserve bottle in East Africa and select global cultural hubs. Traditional liquor advertising relies on generic celebration tropes and subdued, uninspired warmth.

Our task was to break that convention entirely. We engineered an avant-garde nocturnal visual language inspired by Tokyo cyberpunk, high-fashion brutalism, and Kenyan underground electronic culture.

[Empirical Impact]

Numbers that validate the rebellion.

+240%

Social & editorial engagement across launching channels.

1.8M

Organic video impressions without paid booster allocations.

100%

Initial allocation batch sold out within 72 hours of premiere.

"Reigny didn't just give us a film; they gave our brand a heartbeat that commanded absolute silence in every room it was screened."

— Marcus Vance, Brand Director, Noir Spirits
